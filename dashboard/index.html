How a launch is priced.

Farm economics, stated plainly. Airdrop slice 5% of implied value; one farmer per $50,000 of TVL (500–100,000); fees $20 plus $4 per on-chain step; 90 minutes per step. Verdicts come from this board's own net-per-hour quartiles: FARM is the top quarter, MARGINAL the middle half, SKIP the bottom quarter or a net loss.

What this is not. A protocol "records no token" in the snapshot — that is not proof it has none, and this board never predicts whether or when a token is issued. Implied value is this entry's own claim from the snapshot's record. Token odds are the sector's own tokenisation rate and did not beat the flat rate out of sample, so they are shown for context and never used for ranking.
